# FOG-ORCHESTRATOR 2.0 — Fleet Productivity & Safety Benchmarks
**Document ID:** `DOC-06-VAL-04` | **Audited Standard:** Simulation Benchmark

---

## 1. Fleet Operational Performance: Baseline vs FOG-ORCHESTRATOR

A 50-vehicle fleet was simulated over 24 hours of operation under cyclic fog events at NMDC Bailadila Deposit 5:

| Operational Metric | Conventional Baseline (Current Practice) | FOG-ORCHESTRATOR 2.0 | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Clear Weather Throughput** | 4,200 tonnes / hour | 4,200 tonnes / hour | Identical baseline capacity |
| **Heavy Fog Throughput ($V_{vis} < 50\text{m}$)**| **0 tonnes / hour (Shutdown)** | **2,870 tonnes / hour** | **+68.4% throughput retention** |
| **Haul Road Collisions (500 runs)** | **14 collisions** (during blind runs) | **0 collisions** | **Zero safety incidents** |
| **Switchback Queue Length** | Uncontrolled (up to 8 dumpers) | Bounded to $\le 2$ dumpers | Eliminates ramp gridlock |
| **Fleet Downtime per Fog Shift** | 4.5 hours | **0.0 hours** | Continuous safe haulage |

---

## 2. Economic & Operational Value

- **Throughput Retained**: 2,870 tonnes of iron ore per hour during fog periods.
- **Estimated Savings**: Preventing a 4-hour monsoon fog shutdown preserves over **INR 1.8 to 2.4 Crores** in operational value per shift for NMDC Bailadila.
